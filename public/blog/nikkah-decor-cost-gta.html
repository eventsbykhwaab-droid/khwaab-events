How Much Does Nikkah Decor Cost in the GTA?

A practical guide to the elements that shape your quote, what is usually included, and how to choose a setup that fits your celebration.

Published August 4, 2026 · Khwaab Events

Nikkah decor in the Greater Toronto Area can range from a simple ceremony divider to a fully styled hall with a backdrop, seating, aisle decor, signage, and tables. The final price depends less on the event name and more on the size of the setup, the amount of floral coverage, the venue, and the work needed to install everything safely.

A simple starting range

For many intimate Nikkah ceremonies, a divider package can start around the mid-hundreds. A fuller floral divider, larger backdrop, or combined ceremony bundle will cost more because it requires additional inventory, florals, transportation, and setup time.

Khwaab package starting points: Classic divider setups start from $550, full floral divider setups start from $600, and intimate Nikkah bundles start from $1,000. Your final quote depends on the details of your event.

What affects the price?

1. The size of the setup

A compact divider for a home or masjid requires fewer materials than a wide banquet-hall backdrop. Height and width also matter because larger structures require more draping, florals, support pieces, and installation time.

2. Floral coverage

Light floral accents cost less than full floral coverage. A few clusters placed around an arch create a different price point than florals covering most of the structure. Custom colours or uncommon flower combinations can also affect availability and cost.

3. Seating and side props

Benches, a loveseat, rugs, candles, lanterns, plinths, vases, and side arrangements help complete the setup. Some packages include selected pieces, while others can be added based on the space and style.

4. Setup and takedown access

Venue access can change the amount of labour required. Important details include loading distance, stairs, elevators, parking, the time available for setup, and how late takedown must happen.

5. Travel and transportation

Khwaab Events is based in Mississauga. Events farther across the GTA or outside the GTA may have a travel and transportation fee based on distance, vehicle requirements, and the size of the decor order.

What is usually included?

A Nikkah decor quote may include a combination of the following:

  • Nikkah divider, arch, or draped backdrop
  • Floral styling in your selected colour palette
  • Rug and ceremony seating
  • Candles, vases, lanterns, plinths, or side props
  • Setup and takedown
  • Optional imam table styling, aisle decor, welcome signage, cake table, or centrepieces

How to get an accurate quote

Send your decorator the event date, venue, event type, approximate guest count, setup and takedown window, colour palette, and a few inspiration photos. It also helps to share your budget early. A clear budget allows the decorator to recommend where to spend more and where to simplify without losing the overall look.

How to stay within budget

  • Choose one main focal point instead of styling every corner.
  • Use broken floral clusters instead of full floral coverage.
  • Reuse ceremony decor for photos or another part of the event when the timeline allows.
  • Prioritize the divider, backdrop, or seating area that will appear most in photos.
  • Confirm venue access early to avoid last-minute labour changes.
